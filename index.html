About

I work on whether AI agents, and the evaluations that grade them, can be trusted. I build graders, retests and evidence records, and I look for the places where they fail silently.

I am a Member of Technical Staff at AIUC, on autonomous evaluations. Before that I spent four years at Meta on agent evaluation, Instagram Feed latency and data center chain of custody, and four years at Microsoft. In open source I work mostly on OpenTelemetry, where I wrote the generative AI plan operation.

NowAIUC, Member of Technical StaffAutonomous evaluations: checking that the tests used to certify AI agents grade them correctly. Since November 2026.
